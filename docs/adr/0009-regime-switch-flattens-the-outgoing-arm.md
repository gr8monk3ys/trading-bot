# One regime per bar; switching arms flattens the outgoing arm

Two regime detectors with the same multiplier band fire at different layers and compose silently, and switching arms in the adaptive strategy reassigns a pointer while the dormant arm's positions and trailing stops go unmanaged. We decided the session computes one regime per bar with one multiplier, applied once in position sizing, and that an arm switch first collects exit intents for every position the outgoing arm holds. Handing positions over to an arm that did not open them was rejected because no arm's exit rules are defined for another arm's entries.
